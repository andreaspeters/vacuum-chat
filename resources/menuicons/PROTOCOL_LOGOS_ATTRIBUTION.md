# Protocol logo assets

These 64×64 UI assets preserve each source logo's geometry and aspect ratio, place the source artwork on a neutral dark tile for contrast, and are rendered by the roster at the existing presence-decoration size.

- XMPP: official XMPP Standards Foundation press-kit monochrome white logo, https://xmpp.org/images/logos/xmpp-logo-monochrome-white.png (press kit: https://xmpp.org/about/press-kit/).
- Matrix: official Matrix.org Foundation white icon from https://matrix.org/branding/icons/matrix-icons.zip. The Matrix logo is a trademark of The Matrix.org Foundation. Use of the logo does not imply endorsement or affiliation. See https://matrix.org/branding/.
- MeshCore: official white wordmark from https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/blob/main/logo/meshcore.png. Its wide aspect ratio is preserved inside the square icon; at the roster size the wordmark is necessarily small.
